[h1]RimSynapse - Psychology[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Psychology/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Your colonists remember what happened to them, and it changes who they become.[/b]

RimSynapse Psychology replaces vanilla's rigid opinion number with a fluid Trust and Familiarity system, and lets colonists accumulate core memories that reshape their traits, their relationships and their mental breaks - all read and weighed by a language model.

---

[h2]Changelog (v0.10.0) - Living Societies[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]NEW - Relationship Compass:[/b] relationships are now two axes - Warmth (do they like each other?) and Trust (do they rely on each other?), each -100 to 100 - seeded by personality compatibility and moved by what colonists actually do together, not by chit-chat volume. Friendship AND rivalry milestones fire on both axes (so two colonists who only trade insults never become "best friends"), with reconciliation letters when old enemies make peace. Adds an emotional-brake "compulsion control", a nightly relationship evaluation, and an ideology-driven befriend -> convert -> recruit loop.
[*] [b]NEW - Therapy is treatment, not a coin flip:[/b] psychological struggles are now treatable conditions (Trauma/PTSD, Depression, Anxiety, Bipolar, Neurotic) cleared over repeated sessions with a weighted quality score - therapist Social skill, the patient's own bedroom, trust, and how reachable they are - held in the patient's bedroom rather than a hospital bed. Untreated conditions worsen and break more often. Pyromania is chronic (managed, never cured); Grief arises when someone close dies and eases with time or therapy. Three session modes: Guiding Hand, Watch, and Resolve in Background.
[*] [b]NEW - Colony Cliques:[/b] emergent friend-groups form from who your colonists are genuinely warm and familiar with, and feed into their dialogue and evaluations.
[*] [b]NEW - Trait History Timeline and richer leaders:[/b] every trait gained or lost is dated with the reasoning behind it; visitor and world-leader backstories take in outside faction context, with a new leadership "reports-to" hook for future faction hierarchies.
[*] [b]Fixed:[/b] the euphoria trait hook is data-driven - any trait can opt into the euphoric-reckless path instead of the game hardcoding bipolar.
[*] Requires the latest RimSynapse Core.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility Matrix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Royalty DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Integrates memories of nobility and royal power.
[*] [b]Ideology DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Integrates crises of faith and spiritual rebirth memories. Required for deep Faction Leader generation.
[*] [b]Biotech DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Integrates genetic dysphoria and vat-grown trauma.
[*] [b]Anomaly DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Integrates void taint and cosmic horror PTSD.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Factions:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Empowers world generation to build deep psychological profiles and traits for Faction Leaders.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Conversations:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Colonists' generated dialogue will be directly influenced by their current Trust levels, Familiarity, and accumulated traumas.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - WorldNews:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Prints gossip and news articles about your pawns' most dramatic psychological events and relationships.
