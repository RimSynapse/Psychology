[h1]RimSynapse - Psychology[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Psychology/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Your colonists remember what happened to them, and it changes who they become.[/b]

RimSynapse Psychology replaces vanilla's rigid opinion number with a fluid Trust and Familiarity system, and lets colonists accumulate core memories that reshape their traits, their relationships and their mental breaks - all read and weighed by a language model.

---

[h2]Roadmap[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]0.8.1 - Clinical Review Scheduling:[/b] nightly reviews size themselves to the night and finish by morning on a large colony.
[*] [b]0.9.0 - Prisoners, Leaders and Trait History:[/b] profiles for prisoners, slaves and guests, richer visitor and leader backstories, and a dated timeline of traits gained or lost.
[*] [b]0.10.0 - Living Societies:[/b] colony cliques, a rumour mill, friendship-milestone letters, and therapy that can run in the background or be watched live.
[*] [b]0.11.0 - Social Network Visualization:[/b] the Social Network tab becomes a colony-wide graph of trust, familiarity and cliques.
[/list]
These ship when they are ready - no dates.

[h2]Changelog (v0.8.0) - Living Traits, Voice and Clinical Insight[/h2]
[list]
[*] NEW - Living traits: a colonist's personality now drifts from what they actually do and live through, weighed by an LLM judge and narrated back to you, with a feedback loop that learns as it goes.
[*] NEW - Kindness and abrasiveness now register as social signals, feeding new affinity and devotion between colonists - and pawns pushed past their limits develop graduated aversions and their own ways of coping.
[*] NEW - "Forming Traits" view: the pawn's Psychology window now shows which traits are drifting and where they are heading, with new sliders in mod settings to tune how fast personalities change.
[*] NEW - Pawn Voice: Psychology now writes each colonist's narrative voice - their turn of phrase plus a spoken Kokoro voice - so they sound like themselves.
[*] NEW - Colonists now form a memory of the day they arrived, and Psychology plugs into Core 0.8's shared context hooks so other modules can read a pawn's state.
[*] Changed: the clinical evaluation is rebuilt into five dynamic sections with at-a-glance warning badges, and now covers only pawns affiliated with your colony.
[*] Changed: episode narration only speaks up when a pawn was actually involved, uses the right pronouns, and runs as long as the moment deserves.
[*] Performance: smoothed out the mental-break manager and trimmed hot-path costs for less stutter.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.7.1) - Gradual personality and smarter memory[/h2]
[list]
[*] Fixed the headline bug: a colonist could gain Bloodlust and lose a steadfast trait just from repeatedly attacking an object (like a wrecked vehicle). Violence against inert objects is now clearly distinguished from harming the living and can no longer drive bloodthirsty personality changes.
[*] NEW - Gradual, multi-day personality shifts: traits no longer flip in a single day. Evidence builds as "pressure" over several days, is resisted by steadfast traits (which also protect themselves from removal), and only fires once sustained - and an evaluation that calls a shift "unlikely" can no longer flip a trait at all.
[*] NEW - Trait-change guardrails: only sanctioned traits can be added or removed (never, for example, Psychopath), at most one change per evaluation, with a cooldown between changes.
[*] NEW - Mod settings sliders: trait-shift threshold and pressure decay, memory consolidation, abandonment risk, suicide damage, opinion/trust blend, and an evaluation cadence to cut LLM token cost.
[*] Fixed: AI-driven mental breaks now actually fire (the request was previously left inert).
[*] Fixed: therapy-session summaries are now kept as durable memories on both colonists instead of being discarded.
[*] The daily evaluation now separates today from a lifetime of history, and the memory panel splits short vs long-term by real importance.
[/list]

[*] [b]Anomaly DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Integrates void taint and cosmic horror PTSD.
