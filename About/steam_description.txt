[h1]RimSynapse - Psychology[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Psychology/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Your colonists remember what happened to them, and it changes who they become.[/b]

RimSynapse Psychology replaces vanilla's rigid opinion number with a fluid Trust and Familiarity system, and lets colonists accumulate core memories that reshape their traits, their relationships and their mental breaks - all read and weighed by a language model.

---

[h2]Roadmap[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]0.8.1 - Clinical Review Scheduling:[/b] nightly reviews size themselves to the night and finish by morning on a large colony.
[*] [b]0.9.0 - Prisoners, Leaders and Trait History:[/b] profiles for prisoners, slaves and guests, richer visitor and leader backstories, and a dated timeline of traits gained or lost.
[*] [b]0.10.0 - Living Societies:[/b] colony cliques, a rumour mill, friendship-milestone letters, and therapy that can run in the background or be watched live.
[*] [b]0.11.0 - Social Network Visualization:[/b] the Social Network tab becomes a colony-wide graph of trust, familiarity and cliques.
[/list]
These ship when they are ready - no dates.

[h2]Changelog (v0.9.3) - Voices that grow with the pawn[/h2]
[list]
[*] NEW - Daily voice tracking: each pawn's speaking voice is now refreshed as part of the nightly psychological review, so a forming trait, a mental break, or a mood that hardens over days gradually surfaces in how they talk. Voices stay recognizable day to day - they only shift when the pawn genuinely shifts. No extra AI calls: the voice rides the review the colony already runs.
[*] Requires RimSynapse Core v0.9.1.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.9.2) - Voices that sound like people[/h2]
[list]
[*] Fixed: generated speaking voices no longer read like a psychologist's assessment, which was dragging every conversation into cold, clinical language. Voices are now written in plain everyday words and include a sample line of actual speech, so each pawn's dialogue sounds like a person with their personality - not a lab report.
[*] Existing pawns keep their old voice until it regenerates; use the "Psychology: (Re)generate voice" debug action to refresh a pawn immediately.
[*] Requires RimSynapse Core v0.9.1.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.9.1) - Core 0.9.1 Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] Fixed: rebuilt against RimSynapse Core 0.9.1. Psychology 0.9.0's assembly could fail to load against Core 0.9.1 with a Prepatcher type-load error - this build resolves cleanly against the current Core.
[*] Fixed: Psychology now reports its true version to Core's compatibility registry, which had been stuck reporting an old build number.
[*] Requires RimSynapse Core v0.9.1.
[/list]

[h2]Changelog (v0.9.0) - Prisoner Minds and Linked Memories[/h2]
[list]
[*] NEW - Psychological profiles for prisoners, slaves and guests: captives and visitors staying under your roof now think, evaluate and speak as themselves instead of sharing one blank register - the foundation warden conversations build on.
[*] Changed: event and death memories now key their subject on Core 0.9's canonical pawn id, so a colonist's memories about someone truly link up - chit-chat about a pawn consolidates with their later death, as it always should have.
[/list]
